# Product brief and fact ledger

Use for complete creation or missing-information diagnosis. For a narrow edit collect only relevant facts. Do not repeat questions answered in files, current listings or earlier seller messages.

| Area | Collect | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer | Exact item, physical/download/custom digital, count, included/excluded parts | Prevent single-item price appearing to buy a set |
| Construction | Confirmed materials, finish, technique, maker, production partner | Truthful positioning, category and care |
| Specifications | Product size/units, verified capacity, available colors/sizes, tolerances | Prevent unsupported photo inferences |
| Buyer/use | Main use, problem, style, recipient, relevant occasion | Select coherent purchase intent |
| Customization | Editable elements, inputs, limits, font/design options, proof process | Obtain production-ready information |
| Commerce | Price/currency, stock, variant differences, SKU | Never invent price, stock or sale |
| Fulfillment | Origin, ready/made-to-order, processing, destinations, service, packed weight/size, returns | Separate dispatch, transit and promises |
| Digital | Files/count/formats, dimensions/resolution, software, editable parts, access, license | Avoid promising absent files/features |
| Evidence | Listing/export, photos, specification sheet, seller answers, dated stats/CSV | Expose uncertainty and conflicts |

Use the [fillable brief](../templates/product-brief.md). A photo and a few facts can be enough to begin. Unresolved price/shipping may remain outside a copy draft; they cannot be silently replaced in an executable listing.

## Fact states

Maintain `fact | value | source | status | action`:

- **Confirmed:** seller statement, identified product specification or authoritative current record. Retain provenance for business settings and claims.
- **Observed:** visible appearance only. A silver surface does not establish stainless steel or solid silver.
- **Hypothesis:** audience, positioning or keyword idea to assess; never a specification.
- **Missing:** ask or omit the claim; no conventional defaults.
- **Conflicting:** identify disagreement and resolve the affected fields while completing independent work.

Examples: seller says acrylic and photo resembles glass → do not substitute glass. Existing listing says four cups and new brief says one → resolve count before title/price. A pictured flower does not prove hand painting, waterproofing or dishwasher safety.

Missing packed measurements need not block title work. Missing download contents blocks an accurate inclusion section. A suggested category path is useful offline; an API taxonomy ID must come from current taxonomy. Missing maker, price or quantity blocks a complete creation payload. Missing shipping/return choices prevents calling a physical listing publication-ready.

Do not put `[SIZE]`, `TBD` or guesses in fields labelled ready to paste. Put unknowns in `Before publication`. If the seller asks for a fill-in template, label it clearly. A finished approved payload with fewer native tags is a transport task, not compulsory SEO rewriting.

Request business facts, never passwords or OAuth secrets. Minimize order exports and remove buyer identities from general examples. Public competitor data cannot authorize private access or copying. Etsy IDs and MakeBox workspace/row IDs are different identifiers.
