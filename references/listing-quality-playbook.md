# Listing quality playbook

## Offer

Start from confirmed facts: what the buyer receives, how many, what makes it useful, and which choices are available. Keep the photo, title, price, and description consistent. Make it clear whether the product is a download or a physical item. Write original copy in the seller's voice and avoid unsupported claims.

## Title

Lead with the product and its most useful confirmed distinction. Etsy allows up to 140 characters. Fewer than 15 words is a readability guideline. Avoid repeated synonyms, unsupported superlatives, shipping promotions, and decorative emoji. Follow Etsy's current punctuation rules when submitting a title. Give one recommended title and offer alternatives only when they reflect distinct, suitable positioning.

## Description

Use short paragraphs and headings that answer buyers' questions. Describe the exact contents and exclusions, dimensions, materials, compatibility, options, personalization, and care as applicable. Separate processing time from transit for physical goods. For downloads, explain how to access and use the files. Include relevant limits, licenses, and policies.

Before rewriting, note the confirmed details in every section. Preserve the specifications, ordering steps, disclosures, and fulfillment terms. Deliver the full revised description. If it is too long for one response, deliver it in numbered parts.

## Tags

Etsy accepts up to 13 tags, each up to 20 characters. For a complete generated set, aim for 13 distinct, relevant phrases. MakeBox generation requires 13 multiword tags in languages that use spaces between words. Use the shop's language and respect the native field limits when applying seller-approved tags. If facts are missing, say what is needed and avoid filler.

Cover distinct buyer intents using confirmed product type, style, construction, use, and personalization. Avoid near duplicates, unrelated occasions, and unauthorized brand terms.

## Listing settings

- Verify category, attributes, maker, and production partners. Keep product and package dimensions separate.
- Check actual option combinations, prices, stock, and SKUs. See [options](variations-personalization.md).
- Check processing, delivery, and returns. See [shipping](shipping-packaging.md).
- Match photos and download files to the offer. See [media and digital files](media-digital.md).

## Quality and measurement

Mark accuracy, clarity, keyword fit, offer consistency, field validity, and completeness as pass, needs work, or unknown. Record the metric, source, and baseline period before comparing performance. Distinguish shop metrics from listing metrics and compare orders and visits only across the same scope and period. Note sample size, keep unrelated settings stable, and agree on a review window. Treat changes as evidence to investigate and avoid ranking or sales guarantees.

See Etsy's [title guidance](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697), [keyword guidance](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/382774281517), and [listing instructions](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing?segment=selling) for current requirements.
