# Listing release check

- Exact offer/count and physical/digital type agree across title, description, photo and price.
- Claims trace to confirmed facts; no unresolved conflict or hidden placeholder in copy-ready fields.
- Title within 140 characters and readable; complete generated tag set 13 unique relevant phrases, each ≤20 characters; native transport scope preserved.
- Full description retains specifications, options, ordering, access/delivery and applicable limitations.
- Accurate category/attributes/materials; relevant holiday only; truthful maker/partners.
- Every sellable option has confirmed price/currency/stock/SKU/enabled state; no unintended product removal.
- Personalization complete and compatible with the chosen local/direct route; existing questions preserved.
- Physical delivery profiles/processing/returns and actual packed measurements confirmed where needed; digital contents/access/software/license checked instead.
- Seller-owned media match the offer; required digital files exist/open; order/alt text consistent.
- Copy preview and exact intended action have seller authorization within scope; publication/fees not inferred from “draft.”
- Accepted writes followed by authoritative reads; partial or uncertain outcomes separated.
- Seller has a clear next step: paste/save draft, review, approve action, or continue shop work.

The optional local `scripts/check-listing.py` checks structure/limits. It cannot verify material truth, legal rights, current Etsy status or SEO performance; perform the substantive review above.
